# Exomachina, OpenAI Dots, and Grok Bot

**Product evaluation · 1 October 2026 · Positioning proposal, not market validation.**

## Executive judgment

**Exomachina has a credible differentiated direction, but its value is narrower than “agents working together.”** Dots already delegates parallel work and coordinates cloud and local tasks. Grok Bot already supports named roles, asynchronous Bot-to-Bot handoffs, shared groups, skills, and recurring work. Both provide packaged surfaces for everyday research, writing, coding, and operations, removing much of the infrastructure setup Exomachina currently requires. That setup advantage is an inference from the documented operating models, not measured task efficacy. [Dots tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/tasks-and-memory), [Grok collaboration](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration).

The full intended proposition is **owner-controlled agents and crafted services in an open agent-service economy**, rather than only an enterprise review pipeline. Owner vision notes explicitly include custody, no provider lock-in, independently operated public capabilities, paid composition, and both adaptive delegation and repeatable factories. The governed-production foundation is: publish a process, bind qualified capabilities, keep each provider's implementation private, require independently authorized acceptance of exact artifact revisions, and recover work without silently changing its authority or bindings. The adopted architecture explicitly separates **process, provider, and implementation**. This could serve owners and builders who need a differentiated, repeatable method while retaining operation and changing qualified suppliers. It is a product hypothesis, rather than a proved procurement advantage or a claim that competitors cannot develop comparable capabilities. [Architecture principles](architecture-principles.md), [glossary](../CONTEXT.md). Owner vision notes reviewed 1 October; intent and proposed proof are distinguished below.

The prototype is stronger than a diagram and weaker than a deployable production product. A qualified live reference run combined agent-authored publication, parallel A2A research, synthesis, independent Quality verdicts, exact-revision repair, bounded exhaustion, and a Director wait/abort. Release was an HTTP fixture; induced defects exercised repair; reliability across customers, providers, and workloads is unmeasured. Provider substitution, a usable directory, production authorization, real external release, complete Engineering, and a distributable one-install package remain material gaps. [Single-factory results](../prototype/temporal-factory/SINGLE_FACTORY.md), [qualification](../prototype/temporal-factory/QUALIFICATION.md), [prototype README](../prototype/temporal-factory/README.md).

**Recommendation:** test a curated composition with a real outcome owner and independently operated specialists, using a repeated evidence-sensitive deliverable as the first proof. Knowledge production is close to the prototype; software or another accessible domain may be better with a real buyer. This is an entry experiment within the broader owner-selected agentic-economy vision, not an adopted ICP. Prove useful custody/exit, qualified replacement and real delivery; generic assistance alone is a weak initial wedge.

## Evidence and scope

This report compares products, rather than ranking underlying models or workflow engines. It reads Exomachina's current repository at `55e6c05`, including the 28 September architecture principle, the architecture atlas, the dashboard design, and the prototype's results and limits. It also incorporates the owner's product vision notes, reviewed 1 October, with direct owner statements separated from assistant proposals and historical reports. Only product-relevant conclusions are reproduced; private journals, personal context and source paths are omitted. Related memory/context, DSL, feedback, visualization and testing notes remain references or proposed tests, rather than newly adopted implementations. The older engine feature matrix remains a separate substrate evaluation. No prototype, account, workload, or competitor benchmark was run for this report.

Competitor evidence is official documentation and launch material fetched on **1 October 2026**. OpenAI Dots is the product documented by OpenAI; unrelated products with similar names are excluded. Grok Bot is the hosted Bot product, not every capability of xAI's model API. Grok Build's Rhai workflow authoring is a different product and is not credited to Bot. Features belonging to an adjacent API are called out rather than automatically credited to the end-user product. Launch positioning establishes vendor intent, not measured customer success.

The matrix uses these distinctions:

- **Documented:** explicitly described in the inspected primary sources; no independent runtime qualification here.
- **Not documented:** the inspected sources do not establish the specific property. This is an unknown, not evidence of absence.
- **Explicit limit:** the source states a restriction.
- **Intended:** repository requirements or explicitly selected owner direction; specific proposed mechanics remain labeled proposals. Neither implies implemented or qualified behavior.
- **Observed:** a retained, bounded prototype result. Fixtures and narrow scope remain visible.
- **Reported:** owner-described experimental use without independent runtime verification; distinct from a shipped product or market traction.

Criticality distinguishes the proposed entry experiment from the full vision: **Gate** means necessary for credible production use; **Core** means central to differentiation; **Adoption** means important to usability or purchase; **Later** means defer unless a first customer requires it. These are recommendations, not a weighted overall ranking. A stronger app catalog does not compensate for an unmet release gate, and a more formal acceptance model does not establish demand.

## Documented owner vision and proposed entry market

The owner-selected scope is **general agent factories beyond software**, aimed at the emerging agentic economy. Software and back-office applications are possible entry domains, not the product boundary. “Emerging” records the owner's target and uncertainty; this report supplies no market-wide traction measurement.

| Vision dimension | Evidence status / implication |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership and no provider lock-in | Explicit owner thesis: model control and ownership of data/agent operation matter. Export/restore criteria and exit from Exomachina's own future hosted service are proposed operational interpretations, not completed features. |
| Public independent-agent economy | Explicit intent: discover/delegate to public services and compose factories across independent operators, including payments/micropayments. Public means accessible, not necessarily open-source. A factory can itself become a supplied product/service. No directory, commercial transaction or recurring demand proved. |
| Prescribed and adaptive work | Explicit intent: repeatable graphs and adaptive discovery/delegation coexist. Contract-bearing specialists can have private internal factories. A fixed graph is neither the only allowed process nor proof of optimality. |
| Craft | Owner-endorsed value lenses: **distinctiveness, specialization, repeatability**. Factories can embody deliberate context, tools, agent definitions, code and constraints. Strong default models can still produce excellent work; no universal quality ceiling is claimed. |
| Procedure learning and graduation | Owner-selected feature direction: discover useful methods and convert stable work to ordinary software, specialize or simplify as evidence warrants. The graduation thresholds, independent evaluator and rollout mechanism remain proposals/design questions. |
| Smallest useful execution | The owner accepts collapsing roles when a capable agent can perform them well and values optimization through context/tools/model/instructions. One capable agent is a first-class proposed execution option; separate workers or graph constraints must earn their cost. Protected independent acceptance and authority survive simplification; an author does not gain permission to self-approve. |

The resulting long-term positioning is: **own the operation and tailored method; compose qualified independent specialists into useful repeatable services.** Custody, cross-owner commerce and procedure learning are central to evaluating this vision even though they are not yet qualified. A first workflow should test a meaningful part of that combined proposition, not quietly replace it with generic supervision pain. Attribution: owner vision notes reviewed 1 October; maturity/exit mechanisms and proposed market priorities remain recommendations.

## Architecture, UX, and operating model

**Dots is a personal coordination surface with substantial execution reach.** Its chat/Activity experience routes work into child threads and cloud or permitted local environments. Persistent notes support continuity; they are not evidence of an immutable execution ledger. Its local-access limits make environment choice operationally meaningful. A laptop disconnection does not imply that local work seamlessly continues elsewhere. Dots is a serious alternative when the desired outcome is “take this work off my plate,” including coding and recurring monitoring. [Tasks and memory](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/tasks-and-memory), [cloud/local access](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/cloud-local-access).

**Grok Bot offers an explicit teammate metaphor.** Users create roles, converse with peers/groups, share results, and convert successful work into skills/routines. That overlaps Exomachina's organization language strongly. Its personal Bots share a computer and account resources, making collaboration convenient but separating role/context from security authority. Team Bots have personal chats/notes and shared team memory; Slack DMs use requester computers, while channel/group conversations use a separate shared Bot computer. Requester OAuth and shared Bot service credentials are distinct access choices. This differs from the personal roster. Exomachina cannot infer independent Quality from a reviewer label alone; neither should this comparison infer the absence of all enterprise isolation from personal-Bot behavior. [Collaboration](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration), [computer model](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps), [Team Bots](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/team-bots).

**Exomachina makes the process and accepted outcome a first-class contract.** A factory-mode harness uses the same A2A identity as an ordinary capability. Callers request an outcome, not a graph or Temporal version. Internally, an engine owns graph progression; providers own their private models/harnesses/workers; deterministic policy authorizes decisions and acceptance. Ordinary APIs, software operations and waits remain useful; a remote agent is not required for each internal operation. [Interfaces](../prototype/temporal-factory/INTERFACES.md), [architecture principles](architecture-principles.md).

Its dashboard should explain what is blocked, which exact revision is awaiting review, who owns recovery, and which effects are uncertain. The repository floor document is a design. Owner notes separately report a functioning dashboard integrated experimentally with another system and used to monitor work; that use was not independently verified here and is not paid traction or production qualification. The principal UX challenge is hiding engine/build/catalog complexity while preserving an understandable decision trail. A user should see the accepted report and next required decision, while an integrator can inspect the contract and an operator can investigate an incident. [Floor design](design/exomachina-floor.html).

Dots and Grok Bot can continue hosted work when the user closes the app or laptop. Exomachina's lazy local runner requires its host to remain running; an equivalent always-available hosted offering is not qualified. Local orchestration gives an owner direct process/version and operating control, but does not inherit hosted availability. [Dots overview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots), [Grok overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview).

The local bundled runner reduces external provisioning but transfers installation, storage, upgrades, backups, credential handling and incident ownership to the product or customer. Hosted competitors remove much of that setup while constraining their environments. The Exomachina package promise is therefore an adoption obligation, not automatically a deployment advantage. “Local orchestration” also does not mean local-only inference, data residency, or secure isolation from the same OS user. [Prototype limits](../prototype/temporal-factory/README.md).

Dots and Grok Bot also offer automated review of consequential actions. Grok documents a separate action-review model; Dots checks actions against instructions, permissions and rules. These are authorization/safety checks before effects, rather than independent acceptance of a produced artifact. Both are meaningful controls; they do not establish Exomachina's proposed Quality contract. Grok's administrative fleet-operation deduplication likewise must not be treated as task-side-effect idempotency. [Dots controls](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls), [Grok security](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/security), [organization admin API](https://cursor.com/docs/account/organizations/organization-admin-api).

## Proposed target market and audience

The enduring intended market is the open agent-service economy described above. These **candidate entry domains and research priorities are proposals**, not an owner-selected ICP. The following knowledge-production wedge stays close to the qualified prototype; it does not redefine the product. No interviews, paid pilots, willingness-to-pay evidence, market sizing, or validated segment demand are available in this evaluation.

The broader audience has four roles: **factory/service builders** package a crafted bounded method; **owner-operators** assemble qualified providers while retaining authority/state; **specialist suppliers** sell a capability without exposing their internals; **outcome buyers** purchase the tailored result without necessarily becoming engine operators. An agent may discover or request work, but an accountable principal authorizes budget and effects. Sovereignty-sensitive personal operators are potential participants as well as teams; this is not a separately selected consumer ICP.

| Priority / segment | Job to be done and adoption trigger | Buyer / accountable owner | Daily user / integrator | Why Exomachina could matter; main obstacle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Technical knowledge-production teams** | Repeated source-backed briefs, research packets or customer deliverables; trigger: manual review/rework and uncertain handoffs become costly. | Research/operations lead or founder accountable for delivered quality. | Analyst/subject expert and an engineer owning capability integration. | Closest to proved factory: separate research, synthesis, review, controlled release. Must prove real quality/usefulness and simpler operations than current tools. |
| **2 · Internal agent/platform teams with multiple providers** | Reuse a stable business process as supplier/harness/model choices change; trigger: duplicated glue and replacement risk. | Platform/engineering lead; security/data owner participates. | Workflow owner, capability provider, integration engineer, runtime operator. | Process/provider/implementation separation is most distinctive here. The path must work with independently implemented, authenticated peers rather than only project-owned agents. |
| **3 · Agencies and specialist service operators** | Deliver a repeatable contracted output to clients; trigger: more clients and specialized subcontractors require consistent acceptance/evidence. | Agency owner or service delivery lead. | Delivery specialist and technical process owner. | Factory exposed as a capability can hide internal methods while preserving guarantees. Initial one-organization scope and unfinished customer isolation limit immediate suitability. |
| **Later · High-assurance or regulated operations** | Prove who authorized an action and which evidence/version supported it. | Risk/compliance/business owner plus platform/security. | Reviewer, audit operator, integration engineer. | Potential alignment with explicit authority and lineage; current security/retention/auth gaps make compliance marketing premature. |

Prioritize a **workflow with an actual ownership, tailored-outcome or coordination need**, not an industry selected from a broad list. A research team and a service agency may share the same viable production contract; two teams in the same industry may have entirely different needs. Useful triggers include host/provider dependence, inability to retain a crafted method, an expensive failed handoff, or a supplier capability unavailable in one hosted product. Test which matters to an actual buyer; none is validated here.

### Personas and decision criteria

| Persona | Wants | Evidence required before adoption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economic buyer / outcome owner | Useful tailored deliverables with tolerable supervision/cost; may buy a service rather than operate its factory. | Repeated real outputs, review burden, failure/rework cost, time to useful outcome, clear operating ownership. |
| Director / workflow operator | Know what is happening and resolve exceptions without reconstructing chats. | Understandable waits/incidents, scoped commands, preserved artifacts and accountable decision receipts. |
| Reviewer / subject expert | Apply protected criteria to the exact candidate and make useful findings. | Revision linkage, readable evidence, author separation, clear re-review/invalidation rules. |
| Capability provider | Sell bounded work without exposing or adopting another system's internals. | Public contract, scope/price/payment terms, authentication, task lifecycle, context/effect limits and uncertainty reconciliation. |
| Integrator / platform engineer | Change qualified providers and process versions without sprawling glue. | Demonstrated replacement paths, compatibility checks, actionable validation, version binding, manageable owned code. |
| Factory author / developer or authoring agent | Compose and sell useful crafted processes with actionable validation; avoid relearning supplier internals. | A small supported vocabulary, contract-aware feedback, required gates, bounded authoring, authorized publication and active-version behavior. |
| Custody-sensitive owner/operator | Retain operation, memory and tailored methods through host/provider changes. | Useful export/restore, explicit external data scope, qualified worker replacement and documented residual dependencies. |
| Runtime/security owner | Bound failure, access, spend and upgrades. | Install/backup/restore proof, secret-safe auth, retention policy, concurrent fault evidence and provider-specific guarantees. |

### Poor initial fits

- Individuals whose only need is generic always-on assistance, browser operation, inbox help or conversational memory, without an ownership or tailored-method requirement. Dots is built directly around this use; Exomachina currently adds infrastructure and policy decisions. [Dots overview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots).
- Teams mainly seeking named AI coworkers, group chats, connector automation, and easy recurring tasks. Grok Bot already documents that interaction model. [Grok overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview), [routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations).
- A one-off task with no reusable contract, independent review need, or expensive recovery path. Factory authoring/qualification may cost more than the coordination it replaces.
- Buyers requiring certified isolation, residency, compliance, high availability, or a no-admin turnkey installation today. Exomachina's current prototype does not qualify those promises.
- Teams whose existing deterministic workflow tooling already handles the whole job. Agent services should earn their place through adaptive work, rather than replacing working conventional operations by default.

## Differentiation, overlap, and complements

**The overlap is real.** Research, synthesis, reviewer roles, delegation, background work and recurrence are shared capabilities or interaction patterns. These are useful table stakes; they are not a durable moat. Exomachina would duplicate much of the competitors' surface if it starts by rebuilding chat, computer use, broad connectors and personal memory.

**The potential differentiation combines owner custody, crafted methods and dependable commercial cooperation across replaceable providers.** Its value should be demonstrated as three concrete changes: reorganize a process while providers stay stable; replace a qualified provider while the process stays stable; replace a provider's implementation while its consumer-facing contract stays stable. Each needs admission, compatibility, binding and recovery evidence. A fourth test restores owned state on another host without dependence on the original Exomachina control service; downloaded files or a model picker alone do not prove that exit. A2A transport is useful but does not qualify behavior or independently confer exactly-once effects. [Architecture principles](architecture-principles.md), [remote-agent qualification](../prototype/temporal-factory/QUALIFICATION.md).

**The defensibility hypothesis is operational, not protocol ownership.** A small, reliable contract/acceptance/reconciliation layer; usable custody/exit; qualified supplier relationships and crafted methods; domain-specific evaluation; usable incident resolution; and accumulated version/evidence histories could become hard to reproduce together. A generic graph editor, a roster of agent names, or a marketing claim of self-improvement is much easier to copy. This hypothesis fails if customers are satisfied with conversational instructions plus ordinary approvals, or if each independent supplier needs extensive bespoke integration.

**Complementarity is possible but unproved.** Dots has documented MCP plugin/event integration; Grok Bot documents connectors and internal peer collaboration. Neither inspected product source establishes a native A2A participant that can satisfy Exomachina's capability/recovery contract. Do not draw either as a plug-compatible provider. An authorized integration would first need a supported programmatic surface, service identity, task/result model, durable artifacts, access and cancellation scope, and an honest answer to ambiguous effects. A bespoke bridge must preserve those semantics and its commercial terms must be checked. [OpenAI MCP events](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/mcp-events), [Grok computer/apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps).

## Competitor lessons and Exomachina gaps

| Lesson | Product implication / priority |
| --- | --- |
| Both competitors start with a natural request and show useful progress. | **Now:** offer one outcome-oriented entry point and a clear exception view; keep publication/build details behind operator/integrator surfaces. |
| Delegation is already a product capability. | **Now:** demonstrate accepted outcomes and provider independence rather than treating “multi-agent” as the differentiator. |
| Persistent computers, app connections and communication channels make hosted assistants useful quickly. | **Now:** reuse provider capabilities for tools; choose the minimum integrations needed for the first deliverable. Avoid a broad connector platform. |
| Task completion and delivery are separate concerns in Dots documentation. | **Gate:** make accepted artifact, release intent and delivery receipt separate visible facts. Do not overstate the competitors' guarantees. |
| Grok's shared computer makes role collaboration easy but role names are not security boundaries. | **Gate:** prove separately authorized reviewer access and protected criteria; identities and separate conversations alone do not prove isolation. |
| Recurring workflows can be defined and tested without teaching users an engine. | **After first delivery:** expose safe schedule ownership, preview/test and failure behavior; keep concurrent admission policies explicit. |
| Hosted convenience sets a high onboarding benchmark. | **Adoption gate:** prove clean-machine installation, upgrades, backup/restore, and acceptable idle footprint before positioning as easy local infrastructure. |
| Both vendors can expand quickly. | **Ongoing:** date capability claims, watch official releases, and validate with customer workflows. “Not documented” is not a lasting moat. |

The maintenance burden is substantial even with Temporal providing execution machinery. Exomachina owns a restricted language/interpreter, semantic validation, publication/closure binding, contract qualification, A2A reconciliation, artifact/review policy, runtime supervision, an authoring broker, and eventually a useful operator UI and Engineering lifecycle. Those obligations must remain localized in deep Modules behind small Interfaces. Counting engine features does not erase product-owned failure paths. Full autonomous improvement adds protected evaluation and resource isolation and should follow a credible production wedge. [Interfaces](../prototype/temporal-factory/INTERFACES.md), [maintenance requirements](factory-maintenance.md).

## Actionable recommendations and minimum credible product

1. **Choose a real outcome owner and specialist suppliers.** Start with a source-backed report or another repeated deliverable where ownership, a tailored method or cross-provider cooperation matters; independent review remains enforced where required. Declare input/result contract, permitted effects, review criteria, concurrency, retention and operating owner. Replace the research fixture with the buyer's actual workflow before expanding features.
2. **Prove the differentiator, not a larger demo.** Use at least two independently implemented capability providers. Demonstrate the three replacement cases above with stable contracts, explicit qualification and pinned active runs. Avoid promising arbitrary interchangeability.
3. **Qualify real release and uncertainty.** Use a real authorized destination, stable action identity and durable receipt. Exercise overlapping starts, lost responses, restarts, stale acceptance and late completion at the agreed concurrency. Preserve unknown outcomes where a provider cannot reconcile; narrow the promise instead of pretending global exactly-once.
4. **Close authority/security release gaps.** Remove the Director secret from workflow history, complete runtime authentication, protect review/publication criteria and credentials, and define context/artifact access. Establish the initial threat model and support scope. Independent role names are insufficient.
5. **Test the economy without building a marketplace.** Use known independent suppliers and one real buyer; declare scope/price, payment timing, cancellation and dispute handling. Distinguish supplier settlement from accepted task quality; receipts do not imply escrow, refunds or acceptance-conditioned payment. This is proposed proof, not authorization to spend.
6. **Deliver a small usable operating surface.** Natural outcome request; current run/revision/owner; blocked reason; scoped Director decision; accepted artifact and delivery receipt; incident evidence. The existing dashboard design is a starting point. Do not build a marketplace or universal engine layer.
7. **Make custody and the package promise true.** Qualify documented owned-state export/restore, host/provider exit, clean-machine install, lazy startup, two-instance use, upgrade/drain behavior, backups/restoration, bounded long waits and reasonable footprint on an explicitly supported platform. Assign support/operations ownership and retain honest cost unknowns.
8. **Measure tailored outcome value against strong simpler alternatives.** Compare matched representative tasks, including Dots/Grok where appropriate: accepted quality, useful distinctiveness/niche fit, repeatability, custody/migration, supervision, total time/cost and operating effort. Include one capable agent and ordinary software baselines; simplify when they preserve the required outcome/authority. No winner can be inferred from this documentary matrix.
9. **Add Engineering and graduation in accountable stages.** Required incident handling and bounded remedies come first; independently evaluated process improvements follow. Enable proactive optimization only after evaluator protection, isolation, capacity and promotion rules are proved. For procedure graduation, compare the smallest useful code/specialization/constraint change on held-out cases, then use reversible authorized rollout and drift/exception handling. Keep requirements distinct from unbuilt mechanics.

The first credible product claim is therefore: **“Run this qualified, review-gated process to an accepted deliverable with an accountable recovery path.”** The full owner-control/service-economy claim requires export/restore, replacement and cross-owner commercial evidence. Procedure learning must preserve the tailored value users choose, rather than maximize graph size. Neither claim should imply universal reliability, regulatory compliance, provider-enforced spend caps, or production readiness from the single-factory result alone.
