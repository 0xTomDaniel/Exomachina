### Accepted A2A integration responsibility

Complete native A2A support is **not** a selection gate. A reusable adapter is an accepted architecture component. Native coverage continues to measure how much integration a baseline supplies; it does not establish eligibility. Pin the protocol revision, transport and advertised subset, and prove correlation, continuation, artifacts, cancellation and restart reconciliation. Do not assume every integration is pre-v1: Agent Server documents v1.0 JSON-RPC with incomplete methods, independently of its exclusion here. [Protocol coverage example](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/server-a2a).

The adapter cannot provide guarantees absent from the remote service. In E04, a lost acknowledgement with neither outcome discovery nor idempotency leaves an unknown outcome; unsafe resubmission must stop. In E10, strict spending guarantees require enforceable remote limits or trustworthy reservations. These constraints apply across engines.

Before clearing a candidate, retain evidence for:

1. Exact source/image digest, edition, enabled feature flags, dependency licenses and intended product distribution/use.
2. The permitted production startup path without a vendor key, and startup/continued execution without vendor-network access where the license allows it. A development-mode boot is insufficient.
3. Persisted work through restart, concurrent worker ownership, external-task reconciliation and measured throughput at the proposed load. Document capacity settings and paid optimizations separately.
4. Required author/publisher/operator identity, independent acceptance, experiment isolation, audit retention and no direct bypass of any external enforcement boundary.
5. Every paid feature alternative, including its state, failure modes, operational ownership and engineering cost. Replacing a platform or weakening a required guarantee is not an accepted small workaround.
6. Any commercial contract's embedding, redistribution, renewal, expiry, metering and offline entitlements. A license-key bypass or source-visible proprietary code is never an alternative.

Unknown results remain unresolved. A documented production path is evidence of an available architecture, not a performance or reliability certification. Full native A2A remains unnecessary; a reusable adapter is accepted.

## Representative factory

**Produce verified research** accepts a brief, permitted source references, caller identity, and operating limits. A small parent factory invokes the published research factory and receives its accepted result, exercising reuse through a public contract.

1. Validate the brief and resolve approved capability contracts.
2. Run two research assignments concurrently; join their required, validated results.
3. Synthesize an artifact revision and preserve it in durable storage.
4. Ask an independent reviewer to assess that exact revision.
5. On rejection, create a repaired revision and repeat review, with a proposed limit of two repairs.
6. If repair is exhausted or input is missing, persist a wait for an authorized director decision. Expiry produces an explicit unresolved outcome.
7. Publish the accepted revision to a controlled delivery fixture and record its receipt.
8. Return the accepted revision and receipt through the nested factory's public result.

The repair limit, two-branch workload, and concurrency values below are evaluation settings, not approved production capacity. Nested invocation is required. Self-recursion is optional and is not a gate for this comparison.

## Scope and architecture

Use each engine's supported authoring model. The accepted first language profile allows **flexible graphs from approved node types**—assignments, typed conditional routing, parallel work with defined joins, bounded loops including review/repair, Director waits and nested factories. It is not a fixed set of templates; arbitrary executable expressions, unbounded cycles, unsafe fan-out and review bypass are excluded. Share capability contracts, input fixtures, expected outcomes and evidence format across native definitions such as Conductor JSON, Kestra YAML, Argo YAML and Dagu YAML. Code-first candidates such as Temporal, Effect, Hatchet and Restate may use one preinstalled durable interpreter for immutable published documents; count its implementation, catalog and compatibility work explicitly. Their narrow v1/v2 no-rollout proofs are recorded in the round-two result, while general governed publication remains unmet. Assess the complete stack rather than attributing every SDK feature to its DSL. The customized Strands harness hosts both A2A capability agents and factory Directors. Core Strands Graph may build graphs from approved documents, but its publisher, version binding, validation and recovery are product ownership rather than native DSL support. The three excluded baselines remain documentation comparisons. Do not introduce a universal cross-engine DSL merely for evaluation.

| Responsibility | Evaluation implementation | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduling, branches, joins, waits, retries, execution history | Selected engine through its native definition or a bounded interpreter of approved documents | Engine remains the durable process authority; an interpreter must not become a second scheduler. |
| Remote assignment lifecycle | A reusable A2A Adapter or verified native integration; full native support is not required | Remote service owns remote task state; engine records reconciled observations. |
| Artifact storage and acceptance | Durable storage plus a small acceptance Module with a revision-aware Interface | Accepted evidence identifies the exact revision, policy, reviewer, and current assignment attempt. |
| Definition publication | Native engine registration or a Factory Definition Module, plus declared Exomachina checks | Published version binds the definition and required dependencies. |
| Side-effect deduplication | Participating delivery fixture with persistent action identity | Receiving service enforces atomic duplicate handling; workflow IDs alone are insufficient. |
| Fault injection | External test controller and deterministic A2A fixtures | Test controller observes and injects faults; it does not schedule production work. |

Keep the acceptance invariant in one Module so a callback, director command, or retry crosses the same Interface. Place the A2A Adapter at the remote-assignment Seam; confine engine-specific syntax to native definitions or the selected interpreter's Implementation. Extend existing engine history with only the domain evidence it cannot represent. A second scheduler or a second independently authoritative execution ledger would defeat the minimal-infrastructure goal.

Start with deterministic protocol fixtures that implement only the pinned, advertised capabilities. They prove integration behavior under controlled faults. A later run against at least one real agent service is required before claiming production interoperability or model quality.

## Entry conditions

Record the engine release and image digest, free/paid edition, enabled modules, plugin versions, SDK version, A2A revision and binding, database/storage configuration, and worker topology. For Argo, also pin Kubernetes/controller compatibility, CRDs, service accounts and persistence/artifact configuration. Pin the fixture implementation as well. Use the same capability contracts and acceptance policy for all evaluated candidates. Source on a repository's main branch is not evidence that a released binary contains the same capability.

Before runtime trials, record the intended use/distribution model, exact license and edition, applicable quotas, and which required controls are paid or externally enforced. Resolve blocking conditions instead of allowing the weighted score to waive them. LangGraph, n8n Community and distributed Windmill Community are excluded from trials for the current product baseline.

Verify that the chosen A2A version is supported by the actual client and server, including the accepted Adapter where needed. A documentation example using a different wire version is not interoperability evidence. Record an incompatibility explicitly and specify the Adapter work needed; reusable A2A integration is already accepted in principle.

Use an isolated local test environment with controlled fixtures and disposable data. No provider credentials or paid model calls are necessary for the initial fault tests. Proposed defaults: ten concurrent factory runs; two research branches per run; twenty simultaneous duplicate submissions for the duplicate-handling case. Each test records actual concurrency and repetitions.

## Maintenance and improvement extension

The organization has four responsibilities: Director, Production, Quality, and Engineering. Functional supervisors coordinate specialist workers directly, without a separate team-lead or foreman layer by default. Engineering owns required maintenance and continuous improvement alongside production; Quality independently evaluates outputs and improvement candidates. A supervisor can also perform specialist work, while required review remains independent of its author. Apply **M01–M08** in the [component specification](factory-maintenance.md#testing-decisions) to each evaluated candidate. They cover concurrent incident ownership, bounded recovery, recovery-owner failure, artifact protection, trace-driven candidates, protected evaluation, concurrent promotion/rollback, and service-administration authority.

The **Autoresearch Lab is an optional mode within Engineering**. Apply **R01–R05** only when evaluating an enabled campaign. They cover proactive initiation, constrained mutation, production-priority resource admission, repeated evaluation, rejection history, and stopping behavior. A factory with research disabled still has maintenance, continuous process engineering, and independent Quality evaluation.

Use the same capability contracts and acceptance authority for production, maintenance, and candidate evaluation. Record native engine facilities, domain integration, and service guarantees separately. A successful experimental trial does not itself change production; new versions cross the normal publication and observed-rollout path. These additional scenarios remain unrun.

## Evidence and comparison

Every run records candidate/version, scenario, definition digest, workload, injected failure point, observable expected result, actual result, and evidence references. Store secrets-free machine-readable results alongside a concise human explanation. An unrun, inconclusive, or unsupported case stays visible.

For each requirement, classify responsibility as **engine**, **Exomachina integration**, or **remote-service guarantee**. The receiving fixture's idempotency must never be credited to the orchestration engine. Compare the candidates on:

- Required behavior achieved and remaining failures.
- New persistent state, recovery paths, reusable workers, and deployment units required.
- How many places must change to add a factory, capability, or acceptance rule.
- Quality of authoring validation and execution inspection.
- Recovery time under the declared workload, with measurement conditions.
- Edition restrictions and operational responsibilities observed in the pinned setup.

Keep code size descriptive; it is not a proxy for correctness or architectural Depth. Favor a small Interface that concentrates difficult behavior and improves Locality across factory definitions.

## Decision rule

Apply selection exclusions, topology and package rights before coverage scores. Kestra is the observed behavioral baseline, but a separate JVM/database per harness is no longer an acceptable default. Close its shared-local resource, lifecycle, redistribution and authorization gates before product selection. The Conductor source variant remains a conditional technical candidate after a narrow build/restart probe; it still needs a reproducible distributable assembly and matching factory trials. Its documented A2A features do not override the stock-package finding. The [six round-two trials](../tools/spikes/2026-09-22/round-two/result.md) answer the narrow v1/v2 no-rollout question for stable interpreters and expose different ownership and lifecycle costs. None proves a complete product topology or real A2A effect recovery. Strands harness reuse is not proof of a governed outer runtime. Windmill's raw score does not override its excluded Community distribution.

Every candidate may require Exomachina acceptance policy, artifact lineage, service-side deduplication and budget admission. Record those costs consistently. A native processing-concurrency facility is not automatically whole-factory admission or strict remote spend control. Do not reject one candidate for domain logic that the others also need.

Keep Strands as the capability/Director harness. Advance Effect and core Graph to a common product-shaped proof; keep Dagu as the native-document alternative after closure/rights review and Temporal as a reliability comparator. Their incomplete 65-feature coverage is not a zero score. Reopen excluded distributions only if the product requirements, license agreement or assessed build changes. A separately assessed Windmill AGPL source build must establish its own rights and production controls. Keep LangGraph excluded unless the owner explicitly changes that decision. Preserve any unsupported requirement as a visible gap. Record an engine selection only after reviewing actual results.

The evaluation is complete when the native definitions, pinned environment, reproducible fault cases, evidence records, and comparative recommendation are reviewable. The [S0–S4 development probes](spike-results-2026-09-22.md) narrow the choice; remaining package, authorization and production obligations are explicit rather than counted as passed.
