Design space

Each visible design choice records how much information it uses, so the size of each style's design space is measured, not estimated. The page samples 256 keys in your browser. Designs is 2 to the power of the average. Lookalike pair likely after uses collision entropy, which is weighted toward the plainest designs because those collide first: it is the number of houses at which two would have a 50% chance of sharing an identical seal. For comparison, Ethereum's blockie identicons carry about 46 bits of pattern plus three colours. Not counted: the printed fingerprint, wax drips and ink grain.

Ornament is not security. Anyone can copy pixels. The credential would be a signed attestation over these digests, such as an in-toto statement recorded in a transparency log. This page is only its human-readable face.
