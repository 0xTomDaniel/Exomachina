What each part binds

Body text
The Acceptance: Quality accepted revision r2 (af57a60b…) on attempt 2, after rejecting r1.
House seal
Provenance. Geometry comes from SHA-256 of the service key fingerprint, so it is fixed for the life of the key.
Top vignette
Factory version v1, generated from manifest digest a746eb04…. A new version gets a new vignette.
Underprint and corner counters
This exact revision, generated from its sha256. No two certificates share them.
Signatures
Quality, the acceptance authority, and the Director, accountable for the run. Production never signs its own acceptance.
DELIVERED stamp
The delivery receipt, an endorsement added after acceptance: release r2, 1 effect, 1 attempt.
Border microprint
The manifest, package, run-inputs, definition and revision digests, in full.
SPECIMEN overprint
The run used a fixture Director and release receiver, so this certificate must not look like the real thing.
Punch cancellation
A revoked or superseded acceptance (demo only).

Ornament is not security. Anyone can copy pixels. The credential would be a signed attestation over these digests, such as an in-toto statement recorded in a transparency log. This page is only its human-readable face.
